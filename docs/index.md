# Spendidly

A free, open-source expense tracker for Android. Your expenses, categories and chats stay on your phone. There are no accounts, ads or analytics.

An optional AI chat can answer questions about your spending and add or edit expenses, using your own API key from Anthropic, OpenAI, Google (Gemini) or OpenRouter. Requests go straight from your phone to that provider.
